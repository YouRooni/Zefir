# Code of Conduct

Zefir follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Be respectful in issues, pull requests and discussions. Harassment, personal attacks and publishing other people's private data are not accepted.

Report conduct problems privately to [Telegram @YouRooni](https://t.me/YouRooni) or [studio.rooni@gmail.com](mailto:studio.rooni@gmail.com). Maintainers may remove content or block participants who violate these rules.
